# Round 1 demo flow

Use only the explicitly fictional worksheet at `data/demo/fictional_marked_worksheet.png`.

1. Open the **Capture frames** tab and explain that short teacher-marking video is filtered locally for page changes, stability, and blur.
2. Open **Extraction preview** and keep **Fictional demo worksheet** selected.
3. Point out the red teacher ticks, cross, visible individual marks, and the circled `6 / 8` total.
4. Click **Run evidence extraction**. Explain that Ollama extracts visible evidence only; it does not solve or grade anything.
5. Contrast the extracted worksheet-reported total with the deterministic total. State that teacher review is still required.

Suggested one-line pitch: “GradeAssist preserves teacher judgment: it captures visible marking evidence, verifies the digital record, and prepares reliable data for the next steps after grading.”
